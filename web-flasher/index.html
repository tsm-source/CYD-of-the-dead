CYD of the Dead

Install the game on a compatible ESP32 Cheap Yellow Display (CYD) directly from your browser.

  1. Use a desktop version of Chrome, Edge, or another browser that supports Web Serial.
  2. Connect the CYD with a USB data cable.
  3. Press Install, choose the board's serial port, and approve the connection.

On a new installation, the flasher may offer to erase the device first. Erasing removes saved game preferences such as the high score, volume, and touch calibration. Keep a copy of anything you need before erasing.

For connection or board-specific help, see the project repository.
